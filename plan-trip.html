Plan Your Trip

Your travel guide to Cagayan Valley

Itinerary

Sample 3-Day Itinerary (Cagayan Province)

From the city to the caves to the coast

Day 1

Tuguegarao

  1. Morning

    Arrive at Tuguegarao Airport; check in and have pancit batil patong for brunch

  2. Afternoon

    Visit St. Peter Metropolitan Cathedral and the Cagayan Museum

  3. Evening

    Stroll along the Buntun Bridge and enjoy local street food

Day 2

Peñablanca

  1. Morning

    Climb the 184 steps to Callao Cave and see its chapel chamber

  2. Afternoon

    Kayak or swim at the Pinacanauan River

  3. Evening

    River cruise at sunset to watch millions of bats leave the caves

Day 3

Santa Ana

  1. Morning

    Early drive north; stop at the Magapit Suspension Bridge in Lal-lo

  2. Afternoon

    Boat to Palaui Island; hike to Cape Engaño Lighthouse and swim at Anguib Beach

  3. Evening

    Seafood dinner in Santa Ana, then head back to Tuguegarao

Good to know

Travel Tips

A few things to know before you go

Best time to visit

March to May

The weather is dry and the seas are calm.

  • Tuguegarao is one of the hottest cities in the country. Stay hydrated and avoid midday sun.
  • Book flights to Batanes early; trips may be cancelled during typhoon season (July to October).
  • Bring cash. ATMs are limited in remote towns like Santa Ana and Sabtang.
  • Respect churches and cultural sites by dressing modestly.
  • Follow the "leave no trace" rule in caves, beaches, and protected areas.
  • Hire accredited local guides for caves, island-hopping, and hikes.
