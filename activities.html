What to do

Activities & Experiences

Things to do in Cagayan Valley for every kind of traveler

Adventure Beach & Water Culture & Heritage Food Trips Nature & Sightseeing

Adventure

  • Spelunk through the chambers of Callao Cave and Sierra Cave in Peñablanca
  • Explore the 30+ interconnected Aglipay Caves in Quirino
  • Hike to Cape Engaño Lighthouse on Palaui Island
  • Trek to Imugan Falls in Santa Fe, Nueva Vizcaya
  • Bike around the rolling hills of Batan Island, Batanes

Beach & Water

  • Swim and snorkel at the white-sand Anguib Beach in Santa Ana
  • Island-hop to Palaui Island and Crocodile Island
  • Take a boat cruise along the Pinacanauan River at sunset to watch the bats fly out
  • Go boating on the Magat Dam reservoir in Isabela
  • Ride a faluwa boat across the channel to Sabtang Island

Culture & Heritage

  • Visit the brick Baroque Tumauini Church and San Pablo Church in Isabela
  • Walk through the Ivatan stone houses of Chavayan Village, Sabtang
  • Pray at the Basilica of Our Lady of Piat, the "Mother of Cagayan"
  • Tour the Cagayan Museum and St. Peter Metropolitan Cathedral in Tuguegarao
  • Join a town fiesta and watch traditional dances during festival season

Food Trips

  • Eat a steaming plate of pancit batil patong in Tuguegarao City
  • Try pancit Cabagan with lechon carajay in Cabagan, Isabela
  • Taste garlicky Tuguegarao (Ybanag) longganisa
  • Snack on moriecos and inatata, native rice cakes of Cagayan
  • Savor Ivatan dishes like uvod and lunyis in Batanes

Nature & Sightseeing

  • Watch the sunset from Basco Lighthouse on Naidi Hills
  • Picnic among grazing cows at Marlboro Hills, Batanes
  • Drive across the Magapit Suspension Bridge in Lal-lo
  • Camp under the stars at the Aglipay Caves campsite
  • Enjoy the cool climate of Kalahan Forest Reserve in Imugan
